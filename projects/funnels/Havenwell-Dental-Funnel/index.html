Common questions

Know what to expect before you book.

A first conversation can be simple. We will help with the rest.

What should I expect on my first visit?

We begin with a conversation about your history and goals, followed by an assessment appropriate for your needs. Your dentist will explain findings clearly and discuss possible next steps with you.

Do I need to know what treatment I need before booking?

No. You can book with a question, a routine care need, or simply a desire to get back on track. We will help identify what is useful after meeting you.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. If plans change, reach out to the clinic and the team will help you find another available time.

Is this good for nervous patients?

Yes. Let us know when booking that you feel nervous or would appreciate extra explanation. We can plan a visit that feels more predictable and comfortable.

How long does a first visit usually take?

Most first visits take about 45 to 60 minutes, depending on your needs and any questions you would like to discuss.
