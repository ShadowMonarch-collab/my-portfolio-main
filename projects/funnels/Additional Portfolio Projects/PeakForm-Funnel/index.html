The questions guys actually ask.

I can barely find 30 minutes some days. Is that enough?

Yes. Your plan is built from your real calendar, not an ideal one. Most clients train three to four times a week, 30 to 45 minutes at a time. Daniel lost 18 pounds without living in the gym, and that's the standard we build for.

I've started programs before and quit. Why would this stick?

Because quitting usually isn't a willpower problem, it's a plan problem. Weekly check-ins catch a bad week before it becomes a bad month, and your plan gets adjusted instead of abandoned.

Do I have to give up beer and pizza?

No. There is no banned-foods list. You get nutrition guidance built around how you already eat, so the plan survives birthdays, travel, and Friday nights.

I'm the most out of shape I've ever been. Can I start where I am?

That's exactly who this is built for. Chris, 35, was nervous to start and finished feeling stronger, lighter, and more confident. The first weeks meet you at your current level and build from there.
