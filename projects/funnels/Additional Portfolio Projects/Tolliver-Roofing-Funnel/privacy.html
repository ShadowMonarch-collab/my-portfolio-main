Privacy

Last updated August 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you share with Tolliver Roofing & Exteriors through this site. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form:

  • your name
  • your phone number
  • the street address or ZIP of the roof you want inspected

We do not run advertising trackers, we do not build a profile of you, and we do not collect anything in the background while you read the page.

Why we collect it

We use it to schedule the inspection, text you the time windows, and send the report afterwards. That is the whole purpose. We do not use your details for anything you did not ask for.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with anyone for marketing. The only third parties who ever touch it are the services we use to run the business — for example the system that stores the enquiry or sends the message — and they may only act on our instructions.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details for as long as we need them to do the work and to keep an ordinary business record afterwards. When that reason ends, we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us and we will do it — you do not need to give a reason, and it will not affect how you are treated.

Cookies

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies. Anything stored in your browser is there to make the page work.
