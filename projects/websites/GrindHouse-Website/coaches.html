How we coach

Three rules every coach on this floor lives by.

01

Know the name by class two

Coaching starts with recognition. If we know who you are, we know when you're missing, when you're struggling, and when you just hit something you couldn't do last month.

02

Scale to the person, not the whiteboard

The workout on the board is a starting point. The coach's job is to change it, for your body, your history, and your day, without making it a thing.

03

Cheer the last finisher loudest

The person who finishes last worked the hardest to be here. In this room, they get the biggest noise. That single habit is why beginners stay.
