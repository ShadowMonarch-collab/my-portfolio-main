Fair questions

What people ask before their first class.

I'm not in shape. Can I actually keep up?

Yes, because you're not asked to keep up with anyone but yourself. Every workout has a scaled version of every movement, and a coach adjusts yours on the spot. Beginners start with us every single week. The room is built for it.

Will I feel out of place walking in alone?

Most people walk in alone the first time. A coach meets you before class for a short check-in, introduces you to a few regulars, and keeps an eye on you the whole hour. By your second or third class, people know your name.

What does $59 a month actually get me?

Unlimited group classes. Every class, every week, as many as you want, plus Open Floor access. No contract, no initiation fee, cancel anytime. A single drop-in at most boutique studios costs $25 to $35, so three classes a month and you're already ahead.

I've quit gyms before. Why would this be different?

Because the gyms you quit didn't notice you left. Here you train with the same crew, coaches track who's in the room, and if you disappear for a week, someone reaches out. Accountability is the product. The workouts are how we deliver it.

When are classes, and where are you?

Classes run 5:30 a.m. to 8:00 p.m. on weekdays and 7:00 a.m. to noon on weekends, at 1420 Warehouse Row, Kansas City, MO. The full live schedule opens up on the booking page after you claim your free class.
