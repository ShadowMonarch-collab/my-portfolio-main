Privacy

Last updated August 2026

This page explains what happens to the information you share with Grind House Athletics through this site. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

What we collect

Only what you type into the form:

  • your name
  • your phone number or email

We do not run advertising trackers, we do not build a profile of you, and nothing is collected in the background while you read.

Why we collect it

We use it to book your first class and answer your question. That is the whole purpose. We do not use your details for anything you did not ask for.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for marketing. The only third parties who touch it are the services we use to run the business, and they may only act on our instructions.

How long we keep it

For as long as we need it to do the work, plus an ordinary business record afterwards. When that reason ends, we delete it.

Your choices

You can ask what we hold, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us and we will — no reason needed, and it will not change how you are treated.

Cookies

This site sets no advertising or analytics cookies. Anything stored in your browser is there to make the page work.
