Questions about cost

What exactly am I paying for?

A complete coaching relationship: your custom workout program, nutrition guidance, weekly (or monthly, on Foundation) reviews, private messaging, a habit tracker, and a real certified coach who adjusts your plan as life happens. Not a generic PDF you download once.

Is there a contract or lock-in?

No lock-in. We recommend the full 12-week block because that's where the results are, but after your first block you're month-to-month and can pause or cancel any time.

Which plan should I pick?

Most men start on Coaching, our signature program with weekly accountability. If you're highly self-directed, Foundation may be enough. If you want maximum contact, Accelerate adds a second weekly call. We'll recommend the right fit on your free consultation.

Do you offer refunds?

Yes. Our 30-day fit guarantee means if you complete your first month as prescribed and it isn't working for you, we'll make it right or refund that month.

Is the consultation really free?

Yes, for qualified applicants. It's a genuine conversation about your goals and whether we're a fit, with no obligation to sign up for anything.
