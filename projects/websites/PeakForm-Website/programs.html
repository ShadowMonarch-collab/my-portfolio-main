Track 01 · Fat loss first

The Lean-Down Plan

Drop the weight that's been bugging you, without living in the gym or giving up the food you like. Strength work stays in, so the weight that leaves is the weight you wanted gone.

  • Short, efficient sessions built around your available time
  • Nutrition guidance with no banned-foods list, beer and pizza survive
  • Strength retained so you look leaner, not smaller

Best for: men who want to lose 15 to 30 lbs and keep it off.

Track 02 · Get strong again

The Strength Rebuild

Rebuild the lifts, the energy, and the confidence you had a decade ago. Progressive strength work that adds muscle and power, without wrecking your week.

Progressive by design
Programming that builds week over week, not random workouts.
Coached movement
Video cues and form checks so you lift safely and confidently.
Energy dialed in
Recovery and nutrition tuned alongside the training, not after.

Best for: men who want to feel strong, capable, and energized again.

1week at a time, that's the whole trick
Track 03 · Build the habit

The Consistency-First Plan

If you've started and quit before, the problem usually isn't willpower, it's the plan. We fix the pattern first with small, repeatable sessions and check-ins that catch a bad week early.

  • Sessions short enough that you'll actually do them
  • Weekly check-ins that catch a bad week before it becomes a bad month
  • Habit-first structure so consistency becomes automatic

Best for: men restarting after time off, or who've quit programs before.
