The Roof Report.

Forty-five minutes on your roof, written down and photographed, sent the same day. Free, with no deposit and no obligation, and about a third of them conclude that you should do nothing at all.

Book the Roof Report

What lands in your inbox.

Photographs of every problem area
Shot from the roof, close enough to see the granule loss, the lifted tabs and the cracked boot around your vent stack. Not a drone pass from sixty feet, and not four stock images of somebody else's house.
How many years you have left
An actual number, with the reasoning attached: age and type of shingle, condition of the flashing and valleys, ventilation, and how the decking felt underfoot. You can argue with reasoning. You cannot argue with a verdict.
An itemized estimate, good for ninety days
Broken down to the underlayment, the ice and water shield, the drip edge and the per-sheet price of replacement decking. No bundled package pricing, and no number written on the back of a card.
A yes or no on the insurance claim
Whether what we found supports a claim, before you open one. A denied claim still goes on your record, so this is worth knowing in that order.

What the visit is actually like.

Four steps, and the last one is the one that makes people call us back.

  1. 1

    You pick a window

    Text, call or use the form. Most weeks we are out within three business days, and we keep a few slots open for roofs that are actively leaking.

  2. 2

    We walk the roof

    Around forty-five minutes. You do not have to be home, though plenty of people like to be. We knock before the ladder goes up and we knock again before we leave.

  3. 3

    The report lands

    Photographs, findings, remaining life and the itemized estimate, in your inbox by end of day. Printed and left in the door if you would rather have paper in your hand.

  4. 4

    You decide, or you don't

    We follow up exactly once, about a week later. If the answer is no, or not yet, that is genuinely the last you hear from us.

What it is not.

Free inspections have a bad name in this trade, and the reason is that most of them are a sales appointment wearing a ladder.

  • Not a pretext to get on your roof

    Nobody on our crew is paid a commission for closing you in the driveway. The technician who walks your roof is a technician, not a salesperson with a nail gun, and they are not carrying a contract to sign today.

  • Not a lead you get sold as

    Your address and phone number do not go anywhere. No lead brokers, no shared marketing lists, no third party calling you about solar six weeks later because they bought your details from a roofer.

Why we can afford this. A roof lasts twenty-five years, so we are not trying to sell you one this year. We are trying to be the company you call the year you actually need it, and the one you name when somebody at work asks. Telling a third of our visits to do nothing is the cheapest marketing we have ever run.

Worth booking if any of this is true.

  • A storm went through and you have not had anyone look at it
  • There is grit collecting at the bottom of your downspouts
  • A stain has appeared on a ceiling or in the attic, even a small one
  • The roof is somewhere past eighteen years old and you would like a real number
  • You are buying or selling and want something in writing before closing
  • Somebody already told you that you need a full replacement
