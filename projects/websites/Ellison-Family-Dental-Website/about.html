A small practice with deep roots in Rivertown.

We're not a chain and never plan to be. We're the dentist your family can see for a lifetime, and the one your kids will bring their kids to.

It started with one chair and a simple promise.

In 2014, Dr. Maya Ellison opened Ellison Family Dental with a single treatment room, a second‑hand front desk, and one rule she still keeps today: tell people the truth, charge them fairly, and never rush.

Word travelled the way it does in a small town, one honest visit at a time. A nervous kid who left smiling. A retiree who finally found a dentist who listened. A young couple who came in for a cleaning and came back years later with a toddler of their own.

Twelve years on, we've grown into a full team, but the promise hasn't changed. We still know our patients by name, and we still believe the best dentistry feels less like a transaction and more like being looked after.

Independent since 2014
What we believe

Four things we refuse to compromise on.

Honesty first

We tell you what's needed now, what can wait, and what it costs, before we begin. Nobody here works on commission.

Gentle by default

We numb thoroughly, check in as we go, and agree on a hand signal so you're always in control of the pace.

Every age, welcome

Toddlers, teenagers, parents, grandparents. We meet each generation where it is, at a pace that suits them.

Here for the long run

The same faces, year after year. We're building relationships that outlast any single appointment.

Twelve years, one neighborhood.

A few of the moments that shaped the practice you'll walk into today.

2014

Dr. Maya Ellison opens Ellison Family Dental on Mill Street with a single treatment room.

2017

We add a second operatory and welcome our first hygienists as demand grows by word of mouth.

2020

We upgrade to digital X‑rays and intraoral scanning, cutting radiation and skipping the goopy impressions.

2023

We cross 300 five‑star reviews and start seeing the children of our very first patients.

Today

A full team of four, caring for more than 15,000 smiles across three generations of Rivertown families.
