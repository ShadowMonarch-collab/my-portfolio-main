Your first visit

Four simple steps, start to finish.

A first visit takes about an hour. Here's how it goes, so nothing feels unfamiliar when you arrive.

Book

Call or use the form. We'll find a time that suits you, and line up the whole family together if you'd like.

Arrive

Bring your ID and insurance card. We'll have your simple new‑patient form ready, or you can fill it in early.

Be seen

A gentle exam, digital X‑rays and a cleaning, with everything explained on a screen you can see.

Plan

If anything needs attention, you'll get the price in writing to take home. The decision is always yours.

Ask us about costs

The questions parents actually ask us.

Will you be patient with my anxious kid?

Yes, and we mean it in practice, not just on a webpage. We go at your child's pace, explain every tool before it goes anywhere near them, and you're welcome in the room the whole time. If a visit needs to be shorter and gentler, that's exactly what it will be.

Is $89 really the whole price for the first visit?

Yes. The exam, digital X‑rays, and cleaning are all included in the $89, with no hidden extras. If we find anything that needs attention beyond that, you'll get the price in writing before anything happens, so you can decide at home without pressure.

Will I get upsold once I'm in the chair?

No. We tell you three things plainly: what's needed now, what can safely wait, and what's purely cosmetic. Then the decision is yours. Nobody here works on commission, and nobody enjoys an awkward sales pitch, us included.

Will it hurt?

Our whole approach is gentle‑first. We numb thoroughly, check in as we go, and agree on a simple hand signal so you're always in control. Many of our patients came to us specifically because they'd been avoiding the dentist for years, and stayed.

Can you see my mom, my kids, and me on the same day?

Yes. Back‑to‑back family appointments are one of the most common things we schedule. One trip, everyone seen. Same‑week appointments are usually available, so you won't be juggling calendars for a month.

What should I bring to my first appointment?

Just a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of any medications you take. If you have recent X‑rays from another dentist, bring those too, and we may be able to skip taking new ones.

Ready when you are.

Booking takes two minutes and no payment. We'll call to confirm a time that suits the whole family.
