Preventive & general dentistry

The everyday care that keeps small problems from becoming big ones. This is where most of your family's visits will happen, and where we do our best work.

  • Comprehensive exams and low‑radiation digital X‑rays
  • Professional cleanings and periodontal (gum) care
  • Tooth‑colored fillings and protective sealants
  • Fluoride treatments and personalized home‑care advice
Book a check‑up

Cosmetic dentistry

Small changes that make a big difference to how you feel in photos, meetings, and the mirror. Always natural‑looking, never overdone.

  • Professional in‑office and take‑home whitening
  • Porcelain veneers for chips, gaps and worn edges
  • Composite bonding for quick, conservative fixes
  • Full smile makeovers, planned around your face
Ask about your smile
Restorative care

Bringing damaged teeth back to full health.

When a tooth is cracked, decayed or missing, we rebuild it to look and work like the real thing, and we'll always talk you through the options first.

Crowns & bridges

Durable, natural‑looking restorations that protect a weak tooth or fill the gap where one is missing.

Dental implants

A permanent, root‑and‑all replacement for a lost tooth that never needs to come out at night.

Root canal therapy

Gentle treatment that relieves the pain of an infected tooth and lets you keep it, comfortably.

Dentures & partials

Comfortable, well‑fitted dentures we adjust patiently until they feel right, not just close enough.

Fillings & inlays

Tooth‑colored repairs that blend in and restore strength after decay or an old filling fails.

Full‑mouth planning

For bigger cases, a clear, staged plan with costs in writing, so you can decide at your own pace.

Children's dentistry

First visits that feel like a field trip, not a procedure. We move at your child's pace so they leave proud of themselves and happy to come back.

  • Gentle first visits with a parent right there in the room
  • Sealants and fluoride to keep cavities away
  • Kid‑friendly explanations of every tool before it's used
  • Habit and growth guidance as those little smiles change
Book your child's visit

Clear aligners & orthodontics

Straighter teeth without a mouthful of metal. Clear aligners are removable, comfortable, and easy to fit around real life for teens and adults alike.

  • Clear aligner therapy for mild to moderate cases
  • Digital scans, so no goopy impressions
  • Early‑intervention guidance for growing children
  • Retainers and follow‑up to keep results in place
Explore aligners

Toothaches, chips, and knocked‑out teeth don't keep office hours. Call us first thing and we'll do everything we can to see you the same day, and talk you through what to do until you arrive.
