What we fix.

General repair and maintenance for almost everything on the road in West Michigan. Below is the honest version — including the work we don’t take, and why.

Brakes

From $389 per axle

Every brake job here starts with measurements at all four corners, written down as numbers. If you’ve got 7mm left we’ll tell you to come back in the spring rather than sell you pads today.

  • Pads and rotors, front or rear
  • Caliper replacement and slide pin service
  • Brake fluid exchange and moisture testing
  • Lines, hoses and master cylinders
  • ABS fault diagnosis
  • Parking brake adjustment — free while it’s on the lift

Diagnostics & electrical

From $110

The work most shops are worst at, because it’s slow and it doesn’t sell parts. A trouble code names a system, not a failed component — so we test the circuit before anybody buys anything.

  • Check engine light diagnosis, codes and freeze-frame data
  • Intermittent faults and no-start conditions
  • Parasitic draw testing — we keep the car overnight
  • Charging and starting system load testing
  • Wiring repair, connectors and grounds
  • Module communication and network faults

Engine & cooling

From $89

The unglamorous maintenance that decides whether a car sees 200,000 miles. Most of what we do here is cheap and boring, which is exactly the point.

  • Full synthetic oil and filter service
  • Spark plugs, coils and ignition
  • Belts, tensioners, pulleys and hoses
  • Water pumps, thermostats and radiators
  • Timing belts and chains on interference engines
  • Cooling system pressure testing and coolant exchange

Suspension & steering

From $186

Clunks and shakes are usually cheaper than the internet told you. We find the specific joint on the lift with a pry bar and a second tech listening, then send you the video of it moving.

  • Sway bar end links and bushings
  • Struts, shocks and strut mounts
  • Control arms, ball joints and tie rods
  • Wheel bearings and hub assemblies
  • Power steering pumps, racks and lines
  • Michigan pothole damage — a genuine specialty

Tires & alignment

From $28 per tire

We road-force balance rather than static balance, because a tire can be perfectly balanced and still shake. Alignments come with a printout of the before and after numbers, whether or not you ask for one.

  • Mounting and road-force balancing
  • Four-wheel alignment with printed specs
  • Tire rotation — free with any oil change
  • Flat repair and TPMS sensor service
  • Seasonal changeover and tire storage
  • Tread depth reporting at every visit

  • 47-point vehicle health scan with photo report

What we don’t do.

A shop that claims it does everything does some of it badly. Here’s where we’ll send you elsewhere, and we’ll usually name the place.

Body work, paint and collision. We don’t have a booth and we’re not going to pretend otherwise. We’ll happily inspect a car after a collision repair to check the mechanical side.

Transmission rebuilds. We diagnose transmissions, service them, and replace them — but internal rebuilds go to a specialist who does nothing else. Their failure rate is a fraction of ours would be.

Full electric vehicles, mostly. Brakes, tires, suspension and 12-volt systems on EVs, yes. High-voltage battery and drive unit work, no.

Some late-model German work. Certain VW, Audi and BMW procedures need a dealer-level programming tool we don’t own. We’ll tell you that up front rather than halfway through.

Do you work on my car?

Almost certainly. Domestic, Japanese and Korean makes are our daily bread — roughly nine out of ten cars through the door.

Ford, Chevrolet, GMC, Ram, Jeep, Chrysler, Dodge, Buick, Cadillac. Toyota, Honda, Nissan, Subaru, Mazda, Mitsubishi, Lexus, Acura, Infiniti. Hyundai and Kia. Volvo and most Volkswagen. Hybrids of all of the above.

If you’re not sure, call the front desk and ask before you drive over. We’d rather tell you no on the phone than have you sit in our lot for it.
