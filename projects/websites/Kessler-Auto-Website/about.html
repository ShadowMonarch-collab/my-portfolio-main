Same corner since 1994.

Dale Kessler opened the shop with two bays and a borrowed lift. His daughter runs it now, with eight bays and a camera in every one of them.

“My dad opened this shop in 1994 and he was honest — but he was honest the old way, where you had to trust his face. I spent years watching people stand out in that lot trying to decide whether to believe us.

So we bought cameras. Now every tech photographs what they find and it goes to your phone with a price beside it before anybody touches anything. It costs us about twenty extra minutes a car.

It’s the best twenty minutes we spend.”

Nina Kessler

How a shop ends up with cameras in every bay.

Dale ran it the way most good independents do: fair prices, straight answers, and a reputation built one neighbour at a time. It worked, mostly. But it only worked on the people who already knew him.

Nina grew up doing homework in the front office and started turning wrenches at sixteen. When she took over in 2019 she kept the prices and the straight answers and changed exactly one thing: she stopped asking people to take our word for anything.

Every technician got a phone on a lanyard. Every finding gets photographed before it gets written up. If a tech can’t show it to you, it doesn’t go on your report — which turns out to be a very effective filter on things that were never really wrong.

Dale still comes in on Tuesdays. He thought the cameras were a gimmick for about six months. He has since stopped saying that.

Things we do that aren’t car repair.

Free winter checks

Every November, two Saturdays of no-charge battery, tire and coolant checks for anyone who drives in. No inspection, no upsell, no report — just the three things that strand people in a Michigan January.

The apprentice bay

We take two apprentices a year out of Grand Rapids Community College’s automotive program, paid from day one. Rosa came to us that way in 2015.

Godfrey–Lee sponsorship

We’ve put our name on the Godfrey–Lee High School robotics team’s shirts since 2011, which is the only advertising Dale ever agreed to pay for.

Teaching the basics

Ask and any tech here will show you where your filters live, how to read your tire date code, or how to check your own oil properly. It costs us nothing and it makes you harder to cheat.
