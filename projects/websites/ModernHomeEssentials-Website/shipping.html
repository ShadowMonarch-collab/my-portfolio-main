Shipping & Returns

Last updated August 2026

What to expect after you order, and what happens if you change your mind.

Dispatch

Orders placed on a working day before 2:00 PM are dispatched the same day. Anything later goes out the next working day. You get a confirmation with tracking as soon as it leaves us.

Delivery

Standard delivery is 3 to 5 working days. Shipping is free on every order — there is no minimum and no surcharge added at checkout.

Returns

You have 60 days from delivery. If it has not earned its place, tell us and we will refund you in full and cover the return shipping. It does not need to be unopened, and you do not need to explain.

Refunds

Once the return reaches us the refund goes back to your original payment method. Card refunds usually appear within five working days, depending on your bank.

Something arrived damaged

Send us a photo and we will replace it or refund you, whichever you prefer. Do not post it back until we have spoken — often we will not need you to.
