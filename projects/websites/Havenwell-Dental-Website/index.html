FAQ

Questions are always welcome.

Find answers about first visits, payment options, comfort, and scheduling.

What should I expect on my first visit?

You will begin with a conversation about your health history and goals, followed by an appropriate exam and any recommended imaging. We explain findings and next steps clearly before planning care.

Do you accept insurance?

Our team can help you review coverage questions and available payment options when arranging your visit.

Is financing available?

Ask our team about payment arrangements that may be available for your recommended care.

What if I am nervous about dental visits?

Please tell us when you book. We can allow time for questions, explain procedures step by step, and discuss comfort options suited to your visit.

How long do appointments take?

A first visit typically lasts 45 to 60 minutes. Routine visits and specific treatments vary, and we will share expected timing when scheduling.

Can I reschedule?

Yes. If plans change, reach out to the clinic team and we will help you find another available time.
